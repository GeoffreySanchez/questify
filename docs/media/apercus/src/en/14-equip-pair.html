Gear upgrades

When an item that improves your gear lands in your bags, an announcement appears in the middle of the screen. Click to equip it, right-click to close.

With a two-handed weapon equipped, a one-hander or a shield may only win as a pair with the other half from your bags: Questify announces the pair, and one click equips both.

Dwarf Paladin level 14, Loch Modan: Coldridge Hammer equipped, Compact Hammer in the bags, Ironplate Buckler just received.

Equip [Compact Hammer] and [Ironplate Buckler] to gain power
