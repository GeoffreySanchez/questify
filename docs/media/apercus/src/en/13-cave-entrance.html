Caves: in through the entrance

When the objective is inside a known cave, the waypoint first leads to its entrance, not to the mountain above the target. Its title says so: “Cave entrance — The Grizzled Den”.

At the entrance or inside, the arrow points straight at the target. To leave for elsewhere, the waypoint leads to the “Cave exit”.
