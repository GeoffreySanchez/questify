# Questify Vanilla routes: summary

Generated by `luajit tools/routebuilder/build.lua` from the QuestieDB Forever data
(`.cache/questiedb`). Each chapter has its own report in this folder (`<chapter>.md`): kept quests,
dropped quests with the reason, candidate ratios, the itinerary (task by task, with walking legs and
returns to an earlier place), a "Qualité" section (avoidable revisits, missed pickups, split
objective areas, missed passing turn-ins: see `tools/routebuilder/quality.lua`), logistics and
warnings. Simulated times assume a player who walks at 7 yd/s, kills a mob in 20 s (rest included)
and does every step; real times will differ.

Grind = mobs of the `level` steps of the chapter. Quality items = avoidable revisits + missed pickups +
split areas + missed passing turn-ins; every remaining item is forced by the quest log cap (listed with
its reason in the chapter report).

Race paths:

- Dwarf / Gnome: Coldridge, Dun Morogh, Loch Modan, Westfall (through the Deeprun Tram), then the trunk
  from A_01 (Stormwind Harbor boat to Auberdine). About 18 h 57 min of simulated play to level 30
  (1 137 min), with about 167 mobs of grinding in all.

Trunk chapters are simulated once, on the Dwarf path, from a merged state: the lowest level of the
paths that join there, only the flight paths known on every path, and the hearthstone only when it is
bound at the same inn. Every race path is then replayed (`tools/routebuilder/paths.lua`); a step that
is not valid on one path gets a `race = {...}` filter. The validator replays the same paths and fails
on any step that is invalid for a race it applies to.

### Zone order: changes from spec § 6

- Westfall is a race-specific chapter for Dwarf/Gnome (14-16) and Human (11-16): these paths
  arrive at very different levels (15 and 12), and one shared Westfall chapter would have been
  3 levels too high or too low for one of them. Both join the trunk at Darkshore at 16. Zephras Isle
  alone takes Skyborne to 17 (Westfall gave them 5 quests at 9k XP/h), so they also join at Darkshore.
- Night Elves do the whole of Darkshore in their own chapter (12-19) and join the trunk at Ashenvale.
- The Redridge chapter at 19-20 was removed. It gave 1 level for 5 quests and needed two
  crossings between continents (Darkshore -> Stormwind -> Redridge -> Stormwind -> Auberdine).
  Redridge is done with Duskwood at 22-26 (A_03).
- The two Wetlands visits (23-24 and 27-29) are merged into one chapter (A_04, 26-29), and the
  final Ashenvale 29-30 chapter is replaced by Hillsbrad (A_05). This removes two long
  Eastern Kingdoms <-> Kalimdor round trips. Hillsbrad is reached by the Forever boat
  Menethil -> Southshore.
- Bands after chapter 01 were retuned to the Forever quest density: Dun Morogh fills 6-12, and
  Loch Modan, with the two Ironforge chains, ends at 15.

### Known limits

- Grinding on the Dwarf path (167 mobs in all, 404 before the 2026-11-04 review):
  - A_DwarfGnome_01: about 10 mobs at the end;
  - A_DwarfGnome_03: about 68 mobs, to reach the safe level of the Loch Modan objectives (15-17)
    instead of fighting mobs 3-4 levels higher;
  - A_02: about 11 mobs; A_04: about 34 mobs; A_05: about 44 mobs at the end.

  A_NightElf_03 still grinds about 240 mobs (Darkshore alone from 12 to 19). Doing a dungeon replaces it.
- Walking is a straight line inside a region. Regions are split where mountains block the way
  (Stormwind/Elwynn side vs Dun Morogh/Loch Modan side, top and bottom of Teldrassil).
- XP of new Forever quests is an estimate (`est.` in the reports).

### Zone order: changes from spec § 6

- Ashenvale moves to 21-25 (H_03). Its Horde quests are mostly level 21-26, and at 26-28 the
  chapter had 6 quests and a 333-mob grind.
- Hillsbrad closes the route at 28-30 (H_05). This makes one trip to the Eastern Kingdoms instead
  of a round trip at 21-23, and its quests are mostly 24-32. The trip is the zeppelin
  Orgrimmar -> Undercity, then a walk to Tarren Mill. The final Undercity turn-ins come after
  the Tarren Mill flight.
- The Southern Barrens are done with Thousand Needles at 25-28 (H_04). H_02 is
  Stonetalon + Barrens 17-21.
- The big Horde zones keep quests up to 1 600 yd from a hub (`far = 1600`), not 900.
  Ashenvale and Stonetalon objectives are far from Splintertree, Zoram'gar and Sun Rock,
  and 7 standard quests were dropped as `far`. Quests 6 levels below the band are allowed
  (`levelBelow = 6`), so chain heads such as Battle of Hillsbrad are not lost.

## Skyborne (both factions)

- Zephras Isle has enough quests for 1-17. The chain is gated by level early on, so two short
  grinds of about 50 mobs are needed at levels 2-4.
- The exit is written at the end of the Zephras chapter:
  - Alliance: skyboat from the east dock of Valanaar to Dalaran, then the Skyborne portal to
    Stormwind;
  - Horde: skyboat from the west dock of Valanaar to Skywatcher Plateau.
- Dock positions come from the dockmaster NPCs in QuestieDB. Arrival points are estimates
  (see `tools/routebuilder/transport.lua`).
